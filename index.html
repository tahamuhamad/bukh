Who We Are

Heart of the Islamic Community

Al Bukhary Masjid is a center dedicated to spreading the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah according to the understanding of the righteous predecessors (Salaf) of the Ummah. It provides a welcoming environment for worshippers, free from discrimination or bias.

It also serves as a school for nurturing Kurdish children, helping them uphold Islamic morals and the Kurdish values and traditions with which we were raised from an early age.

  • Welcoming environment for all
  • Authentic Islamic Education
  • Dedicated Community Support

Build for the Future

The New Mosque Project

Our community is growing rapidly, and our current facility has reached its absolute limit. To accommodate everyone, we are embarking on an ambitious journey to build a modern, purpose-built Masjid that welcomes thousands, fosters education, and strengthens our community spirit.

Our Vision

Expanded Prayer Spaces

Larger areas for both brothers and sisters so no one is turned away or forced to pray on the pavements during Jummah.

New Classrooms

Dedicated spaces for madrassa, evening, and weekend classes to support education and personal growth.

Sports & Recreation

Safe, Islamic environments for our youth to build friendships and stay connected to the Masjid.

Enhanced Facilities

An upgraded wudhu area, dedicated spaces for teacher training, and expanded community services.

On-Site Parking

Ample parking to accommodate commuters and reduce the impact on local residents.

Why Now?

Over the past 13 years, we have completely exhausted our current land. We urgently need a new premises to sustain our growth.

The Prophet Muhammad (SAW) said: "Whoever builds a Masjid for the sake of Allah, Allah will build for him a house in Paradise."

The Impact of Your Support

Your contribution does more than build walls—it creates educational opportunities, generates roles for educators, and builds a sustainable, self-sufficient hub for future generations.

How You Can Help

Every donation brings us closer to finding a permanent home for our community. Join us in this noble endeavour and help light the path for generations to come.
